# How to Document the Artifacts
## Take Pictures
It's important to make sure you document all of your artifacts. You may need to refer back to them after the session when you are synthesizing your data. It will be difficult to process the data without pictures. Presenting your findings without a visual reference will also be unfortunate, as well.

### What is An Artifact?


### What Am I Taking Pictures Of?
1. Artifacts participants made
2. Artifacts researchers made post design session

### When to Take Pictures
1. Before Session (Circle Time) 
2. During Activity
3. After Participants Create an Artifact
4. During Big Ideas
5. During Data Synthesis

### Who Takes Pictures?
1. An observer, if you have one
2. Researcher(s) participating within the groups
3. Researcher(s) running the session, but are not participants

### Confidentiality
Avoid pictures of the child participants.
