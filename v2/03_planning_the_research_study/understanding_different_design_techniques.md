## FACIT PD Evaluation
Another way to help determine the right technique for a session is the to use the ***F***ramework for ***A***nalysis and ***C***reation of ***I***ntergenerational ***T***echniques for ***P***artipatory ***D***esign ([_FACIT PD_](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/236631644_FACIT_PD_A_Framework_for_Analysis_and_Creation_of_Intergenerational_Techniques_for_Participatory_Design)). 

The framework states that there are ***2*** dimensions to consider for the intergerational participants, ***2*** dimensions of the design goal, and ***4*** dimensions of the techniques. This goes as follows:
#### Intergenerational Participants
1. Partner Experience
2. Need for Accommodation 
#### Design Goal
1. Design Space
2. Maturity of the Design
#### Technique
1. Cost
2. Portability
3. Technology Level
4. Physical Interaction 

#### FACIT PD in Depth
##### Partner Experience
Refers to how much expertise a participant needs to participate in the activity. While all techniques should be able to accommodate all skill levels, this dimension ranges from those who've never done a session to highly experienced co-designers.
